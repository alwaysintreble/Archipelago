(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=(code-of-conduct)=# Code of Conduct We conduct ourselves openly and inclusively here. Please do not contribute to an environment which makes other people uncomfortable. This means that we expect all contributors or participants here to:

  • Be welcoming and inclusive in tone and language.

  • Be respectful of others and their abilities.

  • Show empathy when speaking with others.

  • Be gracious and accept feedback and constructive criticism.

These guidelines apply to all channels of communication within this GitHub repository. Please be respectful in both public channels, such as issues, and private ones, such as private messaging or emails.

Any incidents of abuse may be reported directly to eudaimonistic at eudaimonistic42@gmail.com
